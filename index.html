About Me

I’m a mixed-methods researcher with 5 years of experience across user research, market and audience insights, public opinion research, and social-impact studies. I’m especially drawn to projects where rigorous research can shape better experiences, clearer decisions, and more thoughtful strategy.

I have led and supported projects involving interviews, focus groups, surveys, questionnaire design, cross-cultural research, and quantitative analysis. I enjoy defining research questions, choosing methods that fit the problem, and translating findings into recommendations stakeholders can act on.

My work often lives in spaces where the questions are messy and the audiences are diverse: healthcare access, community needs, campaign evaluation, user perception, and emerging AI-supported research methods. I have also built and programmed online studies in Qualtrics and SurveyMonkey and used Python- and LLM-assisted workflows to speed up analysis and research preparation.
